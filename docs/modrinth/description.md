**The classic sci-fi technology mod is back.** This is an unofficial port of
[Matter Overdrive](https://github.com/simeonradivoev/MatterOverdrive) by Simeon Radivoev from Minecraft 1.7.10 to
**Minecraft 1.21.10 (NeoForge)**: break items down into raw matter and replicate anything you have scanned, turn
yourself into an android, fight with phasers and plasma weapons, tame gravitational anomalies and run a galactic
empire from your star map.

Everything is rebuilt on modern Minecraft, but the original numbers, textures, models, sounds and quirks are kept.
If you played Matter Overdrive in 2015, it should feel exactly like you remember.

## ✨ Features

<details>
<summary><b>🔬 Matter replication</b></summary>

![Matter network](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ahhDxumU/images/bc139fa0e08b38a7420b194152125422aca7b654.jpg)

- **Decomposer** and **Matter Recycler** turn items into Matter Plasma, which flows through matter pipes.
- **Matter Analyzer** scans items into patterns. Every item has a matter value, shown in its tooltip.
- **Pattern Storage**, **Pattern Monitor** and **Replicator** form a matter network: pick a pattern on the monitor and
  the replicators build it from matter.
- Upgrades for speed, energy, capacity and failure chance, plus network destination filters.
- Portable Decomposer, Matter Scanner, Matter Container and placeable Matter Plasma.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🤖 Androids</b></summary>

![Android](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ahhDxumU/images/88939cfe7b5b56feb90b0be689935c6da25430f6.jpg)

- Take the red pill and become an android: no hunger or drowning, energy instead of food, a custom HUD with radar.
- 14 biotic abilities in the Android Station: shield, cloak, teleport, shockwave, nanobots, night vision, flash
  cooling and more, picked from an ability wheel.
- Bionic parts from rogue androids, including legendary ones with bonus stats.
- Charging Station to recharge, the blue pill to become human again.

</details>

- Phaser (stun or kill beam), Phaser Rifle, Plasma Shotgun (hold to charge a tighter shot), Ion Sniper and the
  Omni Tool, a digging beam.
- Heat, overheating and energy packs; colour, barrel, sight and battery modules at the Weapon Station.
- Legendary weapons with rolled bonuses.

</details>

## 🚀 Getting started

1. Mine **tritanium ore** (below Y 63) and **dilithium** (below Y 31, needs a diamond pickaxe).
2. Smelt tritanium, make **tritanium plates** and an **isolinear circuit**.
3. Craft a **Data Pad** (book + isolinear circuit): it holds the in-game guide with every recipe and machine.
4. Power up with **Solar Panels** and build an **Inscriber** for the better circuits.
5. Feed a **Decomposer**, scan items with the **Matter Analyzer** into **Pattern Storage**, then replicate them with a
   **Pattern Monitor** and **Replicator**.
6. Ready for more? Find the Mad Scientist, try the red pill, or look for an anomaly.

## 📦 Compatibility

- Minecraft **1.21.10**, NeoForge **21.10.64** or newer, Java 21.
- Required on both the client and the server.
- Energy is standard FE, items, fluids and energy go through NeoForge capabilities, ores and ingots use `c:` tags.

<details>
<summary><b>Differences from the 1.7.10 original</b></summary>

The port follows the original closely; the few deliberate differences are listed in the
[work log](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/matter-overdrive-1.21/blob/main/MODLOG.md). For example: there is no
config file yet, the machine pages are always-visible tabs instead of a collapsible side panel, and the ComputerCraft /
OpenComputers integrations are not ported.

</details>

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this official?** No. It is a fan port of the GPL-licensed original and is not affiliated with Simeon Radivoev.

**Fabric / Forge / other versions?** NeoForge 1.21.10 only for now.

**Can I use it in a modpack?** Yes, the mod is GPL-3.0, like the original.

**Found a bug?** Please open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/matter-overdrive-1.21/issues).
