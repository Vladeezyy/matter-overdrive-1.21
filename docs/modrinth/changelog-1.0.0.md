First release of the Matter Overdrive port for Minecraft 1.21.10 (NeoForge 21.10.64+).

Everything from Matter Overdrive 0.4.2 for 1.7.10:

- Matter: decomposer, recycler, analyzer, pattern storage, pattern monitor, replicator, matter pipes and network
- Machines: inscriber, solar panel, transporter, charging station, weapon station, contract market, holo signs
- Androids: red / blue / yellow pills, android station with all 14 biotic abilities, bionic parts, HUD and ability wheel
- Weapons: phaser, phaser rifle, plasma shotgun (with charged shots), ion sniper, omni tool, weapon modules
- Gravitational anomalies, stabilizers and the fusion reactor
- Star map with the procedural galaxy, buildings and ships
- Quests, the Mad Scientist and his village house, contract quests
- Rogue androids, failed animals, mutant scientist; android houses, crashed ships, underwater base, cargo ship
- Tritanium / dilithium ores, tritanium tools and the original 3D tritanium armor, decorative blocks
- The in-game Data Pad guide, English and Russian translations
